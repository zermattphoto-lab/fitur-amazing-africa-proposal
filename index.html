Juan Aizpuru × Amazing Africa

Created on the ground, with the people who know it.

Amazing Africa supports travel professionals with itineraries and resources tailored to their clients. I would love to explore how photography and film could complement that work.

Together, we can select a journey or project to photograph and film with your local team, creating material around the destinations, experiences and partners you want to present.

A practical collaboration

Two ways we could work together.

01 / YOUR TRAVEL PARTNERS

A visual collection for a chosen journey.

Follow a selected itinerary, capturing landscapes, accommodation, local guides and moments along the way. Your team can use the agreed material in itineraries, presentations and other channels you choose.

02 / CLIENT PRODUCTIONS

Photography and film for new projects.

Work together on an assignment for a travel agency, brand or other client. Your destination expertise and local network support the production; we agree the creative approach, logistics and scope together.
